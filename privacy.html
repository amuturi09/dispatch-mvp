1Who we are

[Legal entity name], doing business as Dialpatch ("Dialpatch," "we," "us," or "our"), operates an emergency dispatch and referral service that connects people seeking home services ("Callers") with independent third-party service professionals ("Contractors"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our phone line, website at [dialpatch.com], and the partner portal.

2Information we collect

Information from Callers

  • Phone number — the number you call from (caller ID).
  • Information you provide on the call — the service you need, the nature and urgency of the problem, your ZIP code, and your street address.
  • Call audio and transcripts — calls are recorded and may be transcribed (see Call recording).
  • Call metadata — date, time, duration, and outcome of the call.

Information from Contractors

  • Account & business details — name, business name, contact person, phone number, email, service area (ZIP codes), trade, and your per-lead bid.
  • Login credentials — a password, stored only in hashed form.
  • Payment information — processed by our payment processor, Stripe. We store a payment-method reference and billing status, but we do not store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical and usage data (e.g., device/browser information and session identifiers) needed to operate and secure the website and portal.

3How we use information

  • To answer calls, understand the request, and connect Callers with an available Contractor in the area.
  • To screen for safety situations (e.g., gas, fire, or medical emergencies) and direct Callers to emergency services (9-1-1) where appropriate.
  • To bill Contractors the applicable per-connected-call fee and maintain billing records.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

4How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the service:

  • With the matched Contractor. To connect a call, we share the Caller's request and relevant details (such as ZIP, the nature of the problem, and — as needed to perform the service — contact and address information) with the Contractor we connect the Caller to.
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf, under contract, including: Retell AI (voice agent, call recording and transcription), Twilio (telephony), Stripe (payment processing), and our hosting and database providers [Railway, Supabase].
  • For legal and safety reasons — to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, property, or safety of any person.
  • In a business transfer — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

5Call recording

Calls to Dialpatch are recorded and may be transcribed for connecting the call, quality, safety, training, and record-keeping. Callers are notified at the start of the call. By continuing the call, the Caller consents to being recorded. If you do not wish to be recorded, please do not use the service.
